A promise I want to keep

Har mausam mein
tumhare saath

Zindagi hamesha perfect nahi hogi. Kuch din mushkil honge, kuch din bahut khoobsurat. Lekin meri dua hai ki jab bhi tumhe kisi apne ki zarurat ho, tum mujhe apne paas pao.

Khushi mein — tumhare saath. Mushkil mein — tumhare saath. Har naye mod par — tumhare saath.

Main tumhara haath pakad kar har kal ka saamna karna chahta hoon. Aur agar Allah ne naseeb mein likha… toh ek din isi mohabbat ko nikah ke naam se poora karna chahta hoon. 🤍
